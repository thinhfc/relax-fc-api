FAQ

Do I need the internet? No. Every sound is generated on your device.

What happens if I leave the app or lock the screen? The sound stops. Keep Relax Fc open while you listen (you can lower the screen brightness).

How does the sleep timer work? The sound fades out over the last minute, then stops.
